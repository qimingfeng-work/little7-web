HotNews Daily curates global news and hot topics from multiple public
sources (Google News, BBC, The Guardian, The Verge, TechCrunch, Ars
Technica, Hacker News) and publishes short, original summaries written in
plain English.

Each article links back to the original publication. Facts are reported
from public sources; we do not reproduce original articles verbatim.
